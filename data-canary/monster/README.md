# Monsters (`data-canary/monster`)

## Overview
Groups all 15 taxonomic `MonsterType` categories for the minimal `data-canary` datapack.

## Subdirectories & Boundaries
- `amphibics`: [`./amphibics/README.md`](./amphibics/README.md) - Registers 11 amphibic monsters (`Azure Frog`, `Bog Frog`, `Coral Frog`, `Crimson Frog`, `Deathspawn`, `Filth Toad`, `Green Frog`, `Infernal Frog`, `Orchid Frog`, `Salamander`, `Toad`).
- `bosses`: [`./bosses/README.md`](./bosses/README.md) - Registers 8 reward boss monsters (`Apocalypse`, `Apprentice Sheng`, `Bazir`, `Black Knight`, `Bullwark`, `Custodian`, `Dharalion`, `Shadowpelt`).
- `demons`: [`./demons/README.md`](./demons/README.md) - Registers 6 demon monsters (`Demon`, `Destroyer`, `Fury`, `Hellhound`, `Hellspawn`, `Juggernaut`).
- `dragons`: [`./dragons/README.md`](./dragons/README.md) - Registers 5 dragon monsters (`Dragon`, `Dragon Lord`, `Hydra`, `Ice Dragon`, `Wyrm`).
- `familiars`: [`./familiars/README.md`](./familiars/README.md) - Registers 4 vocation summon familiars (`Druid familiar`, `Knight familiar`, `Paladin familiar`, `Sorcerer familiar`).
- `giants`: [`./giants/README.md`](./giants/README.md) - Registers 4 giant monsters (`Behemoth`, `Cyclops`, `Cyclops Drone`, `Cyclops Smith`).
- `humanoids`: [`./humanoids/README.md`](./humanoids/README.md) - Registers 4 humanoid monsters (`Frost Troll`, `Goblin`, `Island Troll`, `Troll`).
- `humans`: [`./humans/README.md`](./humans/README.md) - Registers 6 human monsters (`Amazon`, `Assassin`, `Bandit`, `Hunter`, `Monk`, `Valkyrie`).
- `magicals`: [`./magicals/README.md`](./magicals/README.md) - Registers 2 magical monsters (`Frazzlemaw`, `Guzzlemaw`).
- `mammals`: [`./mammals/README.md`](./mammals/README.md) - Registers 4 mammal monsters (`Bat`, `Cave Rat`, `Winter Wolf`, `Wolf`).
- `reptiles`: [`./reptiles/README.md`](./reptiles/README.md) - Registers 3 reptile monsters (`Crocodile`, `Snake`, `Tortoise`).
- `slimes`: [`./slimes/README.md`](./slimes/README.md) - Registers 2 slime monsters (`Defiler`, `Slime`).
- `trainer`: [`./trainer/README.md`](./trainer/README.md) - Registers the stationary `"Training Machine"` sparring dummy.
- `undeads`: [`./undeads/README.md`](./undeads/README.md) - Registers 3 undead monsters (`Ghoul`, `Mummy`, `Skeleton`).
- `vermins`: [`./vermins/README.md`](./vermins/README.md) - Registers 4 vermin monsters (`Poison Spider`, `Sandcrawler`, `Spider`, `Wasp`).
